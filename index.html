Personal project · 2026

3D Room Scanner

A two-person proof of concept for retail spaces. Record a walkthrough video of a room on your phone, and the backend turns it into a navigable, photographic 3D model using Gaussian splatting.

  1. Step 1

    Capture

    Flutter app with native Camera2 and AVFoundation capture, experimental ARCore tracking, and checks for sharpness, exposure, and motion.

  2. Step 2

    Upload

    Firebase Auth, Storage, and Firestore, with TypeScript Cloud Functions queuing each job.

  3. Step 3

    Reconstruct

    A Python GPU worker (PyTorch, CUDA) selects frames with OpenCV, recovers camera positions with COLMAP, and trains the splat with Faster-GS.

  4. Step 4

    Review

    A web dashboard tracks progress and reprojection error, and serves PLY or compressed SPZ files.

About
